# AMPLYRA AI
## Comprehensive Startup Concept, Product Strategy & Technical Implementation Blueprint

**Tagline:** Your Marketing Team. Powered by AI. Amplified by Creators.

**Product category:** AI-powered Marketing Operating System and Creator Collaboration Marketplace.

**Initial market:** Nigeria  
**Expansion market:** Africa and international markets  
**Primary customers:** SMEs, startups, entrepreneurs, e-commerce businesses and growing brands.  
**Secondary customers:** Content creators, influencers, micro-influencers and niche promoters.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Amplyra AI is an integrated AI-powered digital marketing and creator collaboration platform designed to help businesses plan, execute, manage, distribute and measure their marketing activities from one intelligent ecosystem.

The platform combines two major capabilities:

1. An AI-powered marketing department that helps businesses develop marketing strategies, generate content, manage social media accounts, schedule publications, monitor performance and optimise campaigns.
2. A creator and influencer marketplace that connects businesses with relevant content creators and promoters, enabling businesses to purchase promotional services while creators monetise their audiences.

Amplyra AI also introduces a campaign verification and payment protection system. Creators connect their social media accounts, accept promotional opportunities, publish sponsored content and authorise Amplyra to retrieve available performance analytics through official platform integrations. Businesses receive campaign reports through their dashboards.

Where direct API analytics are unavailable, Amplyra provides alternative evidence-submission workflows.

The platform introduces scheduled five-day campaign reporting, agreed content-retention periods, payment milestones and a dispute-resolution process.

The ultimate ambition is to make Amplyra AI the marketing infrastructure businesses need to grow, while becoming a reliable monetisation platform for creators.

**Core promise:**

Businesses can manage their marketing and access promotional talent without building an expensive marketing department. Creators can access legitimate advertising opportunities, demonstrate their value and earn from the audiences they have already built.

# 2. The Problem

## 2.1 Problems facing businesses

Many small and medium-sized businesses understand the importance of digital marketing but lack the resources, expertise and infrastructure required to execute it effectively.

Their challenges include:

- Hiring a full marketing team is expensive.
- Finding reliable social media managers is difficult.
- Producing consistent, quality content requires time and expertise.
- Managing several social media accounts is fragmented.
- Discovering suitable influencers and promoters is difficult.
- Businesses often do not know which creators have genuine engagement.
- Influencer campaigns can lack transparent performance reporting.
- Measuring advertising effectiveness across different creators and platforms is complicated.
- Businesses frequently spend money on promotions without sufficient evidence of the results.

Businesses need a simpler way to manage their marketing activities and distribute promotional content.

## 2.2 Problems facing content creators

Thousands of creators consistently produce content and build audiences without having access to structured monetisation opportunities.

Their challenges include:

- Limited access to advertising opportunities.
- Difficulty finding brands willing to pay for promotions.
- Dependence on personal connections to secure advertising jobs.
- Unclear pricing and negotiation processes.
- Late or unreliable payments.
- Lack of a professional portfolio showing previous campaign results.
- Difficulty proving their promotional value to businesses.
- Limited access to brands outside their immediate networks.

Many creators already possess something valuable: an audience that trusts their content.

However, they lack the infrastructure to consistently convert that audience into income.

## 2.3 The market gap

Existing tools frequently specialise in one part of the marketing process:

- Social media scheduling.
- AI content generation.
- Social media analytics.
- Influencer discovery.
- Campaign management.
- Payment processing.

Amplyra's opportunity is to bring these workflows together.

It should not merely help businesses publish content. It should help them determine what to publish, where to distribute it, who should amplify it, how it performs and what to do next.

## Pillar 3: Campaign Management and Verification Engine

This is one of Amplyra's most important differentiating capabilities.

The platform must provide a transparent system for managing promotional campaigns and verifying available results.

### A. Campaign creation

Businesses create advertising campaigns with the following information:

- Campaign name.
- Product or service.
- Campaign description.
- Advertising objectives.
- Target audience.
- Preferred creator categories.
- Geographic targeting.
- Supported platforms.
- Campaign budget.
- Number of creators required.
- Expected deliverables.
- Publication deadline.
- Minimum content-retention period.
- Reporting requirements.
- Campaign start and end dates.

### B. Campaign applications

Creators can:

- Discover available campaigns.
- Review campaign briefs.
- Apply for opportunities.
- Accept invitations.
- Submit proposals.
- Agree to deliverables.
- Accept agreed payment terms.

The business can approve, decline or invite creators.

### C. Campaign execution

After accepting a campaign, the creator:

1. Reviews the campaign brief.
2. Creates the promotional content.
3. Obtains business approval where required.
4. Publishes the content.
5. Submits the public post URL.
6. Connects or confirms the relevant social account.
7. Begins the campaign reporting period.

Amplyra stores the campaign, creator and post relationship.

### D. Automatic post identification

When the creator submits a post URL, Amplyra should identify:

- Social platform.
- Post URL.
- Platform-specific post ID.
- Creator account.
- Publication date.
- Campaign association.

The system should verify that the submitted content belongs to the authorised creator account whenever the platform API supports that verification.

### E. Automatic analytics retrieval

This should be the primary verification method.

Amplyra connects to authorised social media accounts through official APIs and retrieves available post-level metrics.

Possible metrics include:

- Views.
- Reach.
- Impressions.
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- Engagement.
- Video watch information where supported.
- Profile or audience actions where supported.

Metric availability varies by platform, account type, permissions and API product.

Each metric must have a platform source and retrieval timestamp.

### F. Five-day reporting schedule

Every accepted campaign should have a reporting schedule.

Suggested reporting milestones:

- Publication day: initial post identification and baseline.
- Day 5: first performance report.
- Day 10: second performance report.
- Day 15: final report for the standard 15-day campaign.

The backend should automatically retrieve available analytics on the reporting schedule.

Creators should receive notifications when reports are due.

Where the official API cannot supply the required metrics, the creator should receive an evidence-submission request.

### G. Creator-assisted verification fallback

Not every platform exposes every metric through its public developer APIs.

Amplyra should therefore provide an alternative workflow:

1. Notify the creator that a report is due.
2. Open the relevant campaign verification screen.
3. Guide the creator to the relevant social media post or analytics page.
4. Request a screenshot of the relevant analytics.
5. Use OCR and AI to extract available figures.
6. Match the evidence to the campaign and creator.
7. Store the original evidence.
8. Mark the report as creator-submitted evidence.

An uploaded screenshot must not automatically be labelled as API-verified.

The platform should clearly distinguish:

- API-verified.
- Creator-submitted evidence.
- Publicly observed metrics.
- Pending verification.
- Flagged evidence.

This fallback should not require unrestricted access to the creator's phone screen.

## Pillar 4: Protected Campaign Payments

Amplyra should introduce a payment workflow that protects businesses and creators.

### Proposed payment lifecycle

**Step 1: Business funds the campaign**

The business pays the agreed campaign amount through an integrated payment partner.

**Step 2: Funds are protected**

The payment partner holds the funds under an approved arrangement.

Amplyra records the transaction and campaign funding status.

**Step 3: Creator publishes the promotion**

The creator completes the agreed promotional deliverables.

**Step 4: Reporting and retention**

The creator follows the five-day reporting schedule and keeps the promotional post publicly available for the agreed minimum period.

**Step 5: Final verification**

Amplyra checks:

- Whether the post is still available.
- Whether the agreed deliverables were completed.
- Whether required reports were submitted.
- Whether any disputes are open.
- Whether the campaign has met its payment conditions.

**Step 6: Payment release**

The payment partner executes the authorised release to the creator.

### Default 15-day retention arrangement

The proposed default is:

- The creator publishes the post.
- The post remains live for at least 15 days.
- The creator submits the required reports.
- Final verification takes place.
- Payment is released according to the agreed terms.

The creator may remove the post after completing the contractual retention period, subject to any longer agreed campaign requirement.

Deleting a post before the agreed deadline should trigger a review or dispute process.

### Payment transparency

The campaign dashboard should show:

- Campaign amount.
- Platform fee.
- Creator earnings.
- Funding status.
- Payment partner transaction reference.
- Payment release date.
- Refund or dispute status.

Amplyra must not independently hold customer funds in an unlicensed wallet or describe a payment arrangement as escrow unless the legal structure genuinely supports that description.

The company should integrate an appropriately licensed payment partner and obtain Nigerian regulatory advice before launching protected holding and release arrangements.

# 8. Social Media API Integration

This is a core technical requirement.

Amplyra must use official social platform integrations as the primary method of account connection and analytics retrieval.

## 8.1 Instagram integration

Use Meta's Instagram Platform APIs.

The integration should:

1. Register an application in Meta for Developers.
2. Configure the appropriate Instagram API product and login flow.
3. Request the permissions necessary for the intended use case.
4. Implement OAuth authorisation.
5. Receive the authorisation code.
6. Exchange it for the relevant access token.
7. Retrieve the authorised professional account.
8. Retrieve the creator's eligible media.
9. Identify the campaign post.
10. Retrieve supported media insights.
11. Store the metrics and timestamps.
12. Refresh metrics according to permitted API access and rate limits.

Possible metric categories include views, reach, likes, comments, shares and saves where supported for the account and media type.

Instagram personal accounts should not be assumed to expose the same professional insights.

Reference:
https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/

https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/ig-media/insights/

## 8.2 TikTok integration

Use TikTok for Developers.

The initial integration should use:

- TikTok Login Kit.
- Display API.
- User authorisation.
- Video list endpoint.
- Video query endpoint.

The authorised `video.list` scope can provide access to the creator's public video list and supported video fields.

Potential fields include:

- Video ID.
- Video description.
- Creation time.
- Share URL.
- View count.
- Like count.
- Comment count.
- Share count.

The backend should match the campaign's submitted video ID against the creator-authorised video records.

https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/display-api-overview

Use Meta Graph API.

- Request the required Page permissions.
- Retrieve the authorised Page.
- Retrieve Page posts.
- Identify campaign posts.
- Request available post insights.

https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/

## 8.4 Social integration abstraction

Create a common interface so the application does not depend on one platform's data structure.

Example:

```typescript
interface SocialPlatformAdapter {
  getAuthorizationUrl(userId: string): Promise<string>;

  exchangeAuthorizationCode(
    code: string
  ): Promise<ConnectedAccount>;

  refreshAccessToken(
    accountId: string
  ): Promise<void>;

  getAccountProfile(
    accountId: string
  ): Promise<SocialProfile>;

  getPosts(
    accountId: string
  ): Promise<SocialPost[]>;

  getPostMetrics(
    accountId: string,
    postId: string
  ): Promise<NormalizedMetrics>;

  revokeAccess(
    accountId: string
  ): Promise<void>;
}
```

Each platform implements its own adapter.

The rest of Amplyra interacts with a standard internal format.

## 8.5 Normalised analytics model

```typescript
interface NormalizedMetrics {
  views?: number;
  reach?: number;
  impressions?: number;
  likes?: number;
  comments?: number;
  shares?: number;
  saves?: number;

  metricSource:
    | "official_api"
    | "creator_evidence"
    | "public_observation";

  retrievedAt: string;
  platform: string;
  postId: string;
}
```

All fields must be optional because different platforms expose different metrics.

Do not calculate a metric when the platform does not provide the underlying data.

# 10. Campaign Verification Engine: Detailed Implementation

This subsystem should be treated as a first-class component.

## 10.1 Campaign lifecycle

```text
DRAFT
  |
PUBLISHED
  |
APPLICATIONS_OPEN
  |
CREATOR_SELECTED
  |
FUNDED
  |
CREATOR_ACCEPTED
  |
CONTENT_APPROVAL
  |
PUBLISHED_ON_SOCIAL
  |
ACTIVE_REPORTING
  |
FINAL_VERIFICATION
  |
COMPLETED
  |
PAYMENT_RELEASED
```

A campaign can also transition into:

- CANCELLED.
- DISPUTED.
- FAILED.
- REFUNDED.
- UNDER_REVIEW.

Transitions should be validated on the backend, not controlled only by frontend buttons.

## 10.2 Post verification workflow

When a creator submits a post URL:

1. Validate the URL.
2. Detect the platform.
3. Parse the platform-specific post identifier where possible.
4. Confirm the connected social account.
5. Request the post through the platform adapter.
6. Verify account ownership where supported.
7. Confirm the post is available.
8. Associate the post with the campaign.
9. Retrieve initial metrics.
10. Create the reporting schedule.

If the API cannot identify the post, use the manual evidence workflow and label the verification status accordingly.

## 10.3 Five-day analytics worker

Use a scheduled job system such as BullMQ with Redis.

Pseudo-implementation:

```typescript
async function refreshCampaignMetrics(
  campaignId: string
) {
  const campaign = await getCampaign(campaignId);

  if (!campaign.isActive) return;

  const posts = await getCampaignPosts(campaignId);

  for (const post of posts) {
    const adapter = getPlatformAdapter(post.platform);

    const metrics = await adapter.getPostMetrics(
      post.connectedAccountId,
      post.platformPostId
    );

    await saveMetricSnapshot({
      campaignId,
      postId: post.id,
      metrics,
      retrievedAt: new Date()
    });
  }
}
```

The production implementation must include:

- API rate-limit handling.
- Token refresh.
- Exponential backoff.
- Retry limits.
- Idempotent jobs.
- Failed-job monitoring.
- Per-platform refresh policies.

The reporting cadence should be five days for campaign milestones, but the system can refresh available metrics more frequently when platform permissions and limits permit.

## 10.4 Analytics history

Never overwrite old campaign metrics.

Store snapshots so the business can compare performance over time.

For example:

```text
Campaign: Fashion Campaign

Publication:
Views: 2,000
Likes: 150

Day 5:
Views: 8,200
Likes: 640

Day 10:
Views: 15,600
Likes: 1,120

Day 15:
Views: 21,400
Likes: 1,580
```

The figures above are illustrative.

## 10.5 Engagement calculations

Where the necessary metrics are available:

```text
Engagement Rate by Views =
((Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Views) * 100
```

```text
Cost Per View =
Campaign Cost / Views
```

```text
Cost Per Engagement =
Campaign Cost /
(Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves)
```

The platform must label the denominator and calculation method.

Reach-based engagement and view-based engagement are different metrics and should never be presented as interchangeable.

- An agent can analyse payments, but cannot independently release money.
- An agent can flag suspicious evidence, but disputes should follow an auditable human review process.

Use structured tool calls, permission checks and event logs.

```text
Campaign Amount: ₦50,000

Business Funding: ₦50,000

Creator Net Earnings: ₦45,000
```

This is an illustrative 10% platform fee, not a final pricing decision.

Amplyra should not describe itself as holding escrow funds unless the legal and operational arrangement genuinely provides escrow.

# 23. Product Principles

The following principles should guide all product and engineering decisions:

**1. Trust before automation**

A feature that produces inaccurate metrics or unreliable payment outcomes is worse than a transparent manual process.

**2. Official integrations first**

Use supported APIs and permission-based access. Do not depend on scraping, password collection or unauthorised screen automation.

**3. Transparency in analytics**

Always identify where a metric came from, when it was retrieved and whether it is independently API-verified or creator-submitted evidence.

**4. Creator-first monetisation**

Creators should be able to access genuine paid opportunities without unnecessary entry barriers.

**5. Business accountability**

Businesses should see clear deliverables, campaign progress, spending and available results.

**6. Human control over sensitive decisions**

AI can recommend, draft, analyse and flag. Users and authorised administrators retain control over important publishing, contractual and financial decisions.

**7. Build progressively**

Do not build every ambitious feature before validating the first commercial workflows.
